## Review Focus
- **Metering idempotency + margin:** a job is charged once (retry/resume → no double charge); credits = ceil(cost×margin×scale); ledger stores both cost + credits. Owned by T2.
- **Overage/budget enforcement:** soft→accrue+keep running; hard→block new consequential jobs at 0 balance; per-helper budget pauses the helper. Owned by T3.
- **Payment-provider optionality:** with `ManualProvider`, subscriptions/topups work offline (no Stripe); the port is swappable. Owned by T4.
- **Account-pool threshold switch:** the pool picks a `ready` account with headroom; at the warn threshold an account is `near_limit` and skipped; on hard limit → `cooling`; no external call in tests. Owned by T5.
- **Marketplace isolation + install:** the catalog is global (indexed from local YAML), installing a piece creates a per-company Connector; a company only sees its own installed connectors. Owned by T6/T7.
