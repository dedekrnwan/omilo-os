# Pricing & plans (B2C + B2B)

Copied from the project doc "pricing-plans-b2c-b2b" (agreed 15 Oct 2026). Prices are hypotheses
to validate with the first 5–10 buyers.

## Principles
- Residents (warga) never pay to use the app; they only pay a small fee per payment.
- B2C (self-serve RT/RW) = adoption + payment volume. B2B (developers, townships, building managers) = scale.
- B2B bundles B2C plans for the RTs inside the estate.
- No fake discounts. The only discount: yearly billing (pay 10 months, get 12).
- Never sell deferred features (UMKM portal, PPOB commission, auto payout).
- Fee per payment drops as the tier goes up. Exact amounts TBD once gateway costs are confirmed.
