# Goals — one-person company mode

**North star:** default-alive. Recurring revenue covers infrastructure, tools and a basic founder
salary, so raising money becomes optional.

The machine-readable version of these targets lives in `state/goals.json` (the dashboard reads it).

## Months 0–3: launch one community and make it work
- Barokah Residence live (target ~1,232 residents).
- ≥ 60% of dues paid digitally (QRIS/VA) within 2 months of launch.
- Real gateway cost per payment confirmed; fee per payment set for each tier.
- Build only what Barokah needs. Deferred list stays frozen: PPOB, UMKM, lending, ERP export.

## Months 3–6: prove someone will pay
- 10 paying RT/RWs on Plus or Pro.
- 1 B2B paid pilot signed (3 months on Plus, one cluster, Rp 2.500/unit).
- MRR Rp 5 juta.

## Months 6–12: default-alive
- 30–50 paying RTs + 1–2 B2B contracts.
- MRR Rp 15–25 juta (replace with real monthly costs + salary).
- Monthly churn < 3%.

## Operating rules
- B2C is self-serve: onboarding, billing and support via the app, Sobi and the help center.
- B2B is the only hands-on sales.
- Fixed split: 3 days build, 2 days sell/support.
- No custom work unless a signed contract pays for it.
- If support > 5 hours/week, fix the product before selling more.
