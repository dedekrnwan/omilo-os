# Brand voice

Source of truth for copy: `../landing/content/landing-copy.id.md`.

- Language: Bahasa Indonesia first (English only for investors/partners who need it).
- Tone: **hangat, jelas, dapat dipercaya.** Talk like a helpful neighbour who is good with tech, not a startup.
- Audience: pengurus RT/RW (decision makers, often 40–60 years old, busy volunteers) and warga.
- Lead with the pengurus' pain: tunggakan iuran, buku kas manual, grup WA yang berantakan, transparansi.
- Mascot: Sobi (asisten di aplikasi).
- Colors: teal #16B38C, navy #0A1326, mint #E8FDF6, slate #415974.

## Never
- No em dashes.
- No invented stats, customers, testimonials or quotes.
- No "coming soon" features presented as live (honesty gate).
- No hype words: revolusioner, terbaik no. 1, disruptif.
- No fake discounts or countdown pressure.
