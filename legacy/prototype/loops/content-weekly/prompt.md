# Loop: content-weekly (marketing, Mondays)

Goal: a week of honest, useful content that brings pengurus RT/RW to "Buat Komunitas".

1. Read `company/brand-voice.md`, `company/goals.md`, `company/pricing.md`, and the last 4 weeks of
   `marketing/calendar/` (if present) so you don't repeat topics.
2. Pick one theme for the week tied to the current goal (e.g. launch at Barokah Residence, transparansi kas RT,
   iuran digital lewat QRIS). Use real product features only. Check the honesty gate.
3. Draft, in Bahasa Indonesia:
   - 3 Instagram feed posts (caption + visual brief),
   - 2 TikTok/Reels scripts (≤ 45 s, hook in first 3 s, shot list),
   - 1 WhatsApp broadcast for pengurus groups (≤ 400 chars),
   - 1 blog/SEO article outline targeting a real search intent (e.g. "cara kelola iuran RT").
   One proposal file per piece (`type: content`), with a suggested posting day and time (WIB).
4. Write the week plan to `marketing/calendar/<YYYY>-W<week>.md` (create folders if needed).
5. Executing approved items: copy the final text to `marketing/approved/<date>-<slug>.md`. Do not post anywhere.

No invented numbers, customers or testimonials. No em dashes.
